ABOUT US

Our Journey Since 1992

Lalit Enterprises was established in 1992 in Jalgaon, Maharashtra, with a two-colour flexographic printing machine, starting from the ground floor of our home. Over the years, we grew, expanded our capabilities and infrastructure, and today we are a trusted manufacturer of high-quality laminated packaging solutions.

OUR JOURNEY

1992Started from home with 2 colour flexo printing machine. Initial focus on bakery packaging.
1992Shifted to MIDC in a rented shed. Added cutting sealing machine and 4 colour flexo printing machine.
1998Moved to our own building at M-189, MIDC, Jalgaon. Added 2 PP extruders, automatic cutting & sealing machine and 6 colour flexo printing machine.
2000sIntroduced 6 colour rotogravure printing (later upgraded to 7 colour), solvent base lamination, slitting with web guiding, XL centre seal and three side seal pouching machines.
2016–17Due to plastic bag restrictions, stopped PP extrusion and flexo printing. Shifted to flexible laminates for industrial and food industry.
TodaySupplying multicolour printed laminated pouches and rolls to food, washing powder, fertilisers and other industries.
